# 02 Business Problem

## Purpose

Explain why rising 30-day readmissions are a strategic clinical, operational, and financial issue for NHN.

## Content To Prepare

- Historical readmission trend from the engagement packet.
- Clinical implications of avoidable readmissions.
- Operational impact on staff, inpatient capacity, patient flow, and care coordination.
- Financial impact, including the stated estimate of more than $42 million in annual readmission-related cost.
- Executive working theories to test.

## Evidence Needed

- Dataset-level readmission rate.
- Readmission rate by diagnosis, admission type, discharge disposition, chronic condition burden, prior admissions, and follow-up status.
- Cost exposure from the financial dataset.
