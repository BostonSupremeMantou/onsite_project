# Business Problem Findings

## What The Board Needs To Understand

NHN's engagement packet frames rising readmissions as a clinical, operational, and financial problem. Historical readmission rates rose from 14.2% in 2022 to 18.4% in 2025.

## Dataset Evidence

- The cleaned analytical sample has 12,000 patients.
- The sample readmission rate is 43.2%.
- Emergency admissions show a readmission rate of 50.9%.
- Unknown follow-up status has a readmission rate of 60.3%, compared with 37.5% for completed follow-up.
- Total care cost in the cleaned dataset is $303.1M and nonnegative CMS penalty exposure is $21.2M.
