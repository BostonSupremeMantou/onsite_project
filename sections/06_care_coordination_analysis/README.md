# 06 Care Coordination Analysis

## Purpose

Evaluate which post-discharge support services appear most effective and where NHN should improve or expand care coordination.

## Content To Prepare

- Care coordinator assignment effectiveness.
- Follow-up completion effect.
- Medication review effect.
- Home health referral effect.
- Transportation assistance effect.
- Post-discharge call effect.
- Intervention expansion or redesign recommendations.

## Evidence Needed

- Readmission rate by each intervention.
- Readmission rate by intervention count.
- Follow-up timing relationship with readmission.
- Segment-specific intervention effectiveness for high-risk groups.

## Folder Use

- `analysis/`: intervention analysis and subgroup summaries.
- `data/`: intervention-level tables and patient segment extracts.
- `images/`: intervention impact visuals and care coordination charts.
- `notes/`: recommendation rationale and implementation notes.
