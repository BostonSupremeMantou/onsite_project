# Executive Summary Findings

## Board-Level Message

NHN's readmission challenge is clinically important, operationally disruptive, and financially material. The cleaned analytical dataset contains 12,000 matched patient records and a 30-day readmission rate of 43.2%.

## Key Findings

- Highest observed clinical risk appears among the `5+` chronic condition group, with a readmission rate of 62.3%.
- Patients with `3+` prior admissions have a readmission rate of 57.0%.
- `Skilled Nursing` discharges have a readmission rate of 54.4%.
- The expanded clinical logistic model reached ROC-AUC 0.74 on the holdout set.
- The expected ROI scenario estimates 518 avoided readmissions and $8.8M net savings before final implementation cost validation.

## Recommended Executive Priorities

1. Deploy a pre-discharge readmission risk score for risk stratification.
2. Prioritize enhanced discharge review for emergency admissions and skilled nursing discharges.
3. Close follow-up documentation and completion gaps.

## Caveat

These findings use the supplied project data and should be treated as an analytical planning baseline. Care coordination comparisons are observed associations and should not be interpreted as causal effects without additional design or adjustment.
