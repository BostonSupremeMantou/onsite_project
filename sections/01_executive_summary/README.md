# 01 Executive Summary

## Purpose

Summarize the consulting engagement for executives who may not read the full analysis.

## Content To Prepare

- One-paragraph business problem.
- Most important clinical, operational, and financial findings.
- Three highest-priority recommendations.
- Expected clinical impact.
- Estimated financial value.
- Expected organizational impact.

## Evidence Needed

- Overall readmission rate.
- Highest-risk patient segments.
- Most effective care coordination interventions.
- Current cost and penalty exposure.
- Conservative, expected, and optimistic ROI scenarios.

## Folder Use

- `analysis/`: final executive finding synthesis.
- `data/`: KPI tables and final summary metrics.
- `images/`: executive summary visuals or KPI cards.
- `notes/`: slide script, recommendation wording, and board-level talking points.
