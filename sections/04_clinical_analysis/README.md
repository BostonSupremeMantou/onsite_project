# 04 Clinical Analysis

## Purpose

Identify clinical and patient-level drivers associated with 30-day readmission risk.

## Content To Prepare

- Patient demographics.
- Diagnosis categories.
- Chronic disease burden.
- Prior admissions.
- Length of stay.
- Emergency department utilization.
- Discharge disposition.
- Readmission patterns.
- High-risk patient profiles.

## Evidence Needed

- Readmission rate by age group.
- Readmission rate by diagnosis.
- Readmission rate by chronic condition bucket.
- Readmission rate by prior admission bucket.
- Readmission rate by ED visit bucket.
- Readmission rate by discharge disposition.
- Length of stay distributions.

## Folder Use

- `analysis/`: clinical EDA outputs and interpretation.
- `data/`: segment-level summary tables.
- `images/`: clinical charts and risk profile visuals.
- `notes/`: clinical interpretation and slide talking points.
