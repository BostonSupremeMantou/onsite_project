# Instructions

Following opening on Friday, students will work in their assigned teams and act as a member of a professional data science consulting firm. Each team will select one of the attached four industry consulting engagements: Healthcare, Retail, Financial Services, or Energy, based on the area that interests them most.

Each consulting package contains everything needed to complete the project, including a client engagement packet, multiple datasets, a data dictionary, and final presentation requirements. Students will analyze the data, perform exploratory data analysis, develop predictive models where appropriate, create executive dashboards using Tableau or Power BI, evaluate the financial and operational impact of their findings, and develop strategic recommendations.

Throughout the weekend, teams will work collaboratively to solve a realistic business problem using data science and analytics. On Sunday, each team will deliver an executive presentation to a simulated Board of Directors, presenting their findings, recommendations, implementation strategy, and expected organizational impact.

The daily deliverable for Friday and Saturday is a summary reflection written and submitted by students discussing what they did that particular day. The Sunday deliverable will be the Executive Presentation.
