# Project Charter Checklist

- [ ] Write business problem statement.
- [ ] Define engagement objectives.
- [ ] Identify executive sponsor and stakeholders.
- [ ] Define in-scope analyses and out-of-scope items.
- [ ] List initial business hypotheses.
- [ ] Define clinical, financial, operational, and analytics success metrics.
- [ ] List anticipated risks and mitigation actions.
- [ ] Assign team roles and responsibilities.
- [ ] Move final charter to `final/`.
