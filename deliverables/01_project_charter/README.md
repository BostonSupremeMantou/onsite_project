# Deliverable 1: Project Charter

## Purpose

Define the consulting engagement scope, business problem, objectives, stakeholders, risks, success metrics, and team responsibilities.

## Required Contents

- Business problem statement.
- Project objectives.
- Stakeholders.
- Scope of work.
- Initial business hypotheses.
- Success metrics.
- Anticipated risks.
- Team roles and responsibilities.

## Recommended Supporting Inputs

- `PROJECT_SUMMARY.md`
- `checklist.md`
- `instructions.md`
- `DATA_CLEANING_SUMMARY.md`

## Output Target

Create a concise consulting-style charter in `final/` after the draft is reviewed.
