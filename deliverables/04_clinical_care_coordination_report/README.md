# Deliverable 4: Clinical And Care Coordination Analysis Report

## Purpose

Evaluate patient outcomes, high-risk clinical segments, and the effectiveness of current care coordination interventions.

## Required Contents

- High-risk patient populations.
- Readmission trends.
- Diagnosis categories.
- Chronic disease burden.
- Care coordinator effectiveness.
- Follow-up completion.
- Medication review.
- Home health referral.
- Transportation assistance.
- Post-discharge communication.
- Recommendations for interventions to expand or redesign.

## Recommended Supporting Inputs

- `sections/04_clinical_analysis/`
- `sections/06_care_coordination_analysis/`
- `data/processed/nhn_patient_level_analysis.csv`

## Output Target

Create a combined clinical and care coordination report in `final/`.
